The house

A movement that started as a simple idea — and became Karachi’s new social frequency.

Founded by Usman Ahmad, 12Xperience exists so young Karachi can gather in the open: inclusive, safe, and unforgettable rooms where people dance, mingle, and express themselves without the risks of underground nightlife.

“This is about creating a space where people feel safe. Without alcohol, without drugs, without chaos.” Usman Ahmad, Founder

Crowd numbers are capped. Venues are approved. The music knows when to end. Guardrails are the point — without them, you recreate the same risks people came to leave behind.

Experiences

Six ways the night can feel.

Community nights for a curated list of attendees — and private worlds for brands who would rather be felt than merely seen.

01

Neon Padel

Padelverse, Karachi

Glowing courts. A DJ booth. Coffee in hand. Our neon padel nights turn Pakistan’s largest padel house into a legal, luminous social — the event Reuters photographed in November 2025, with DJ Sagar on the decks.

02

Coffee Raves

Cafés & partner rooms

Specialty coffee, iced tea, and a room that knows when to stop. From Café Ammos to intimate rooms across the city, these nights are designed to end on time — and still feel like a world.

03

Nitro Night

Community edition 09

Hundreds of people. Live DJ sets. Autocross energy, FIFA and racing sims, and brands woven into the night rather than taped onto it — Tapmad, Tapshop, FOGG, ZAX Boost, Aalishan Travels, Lemonacho.

04

Private Worlds

By invitation

Standalone experiences for product launches, equity-building, content, and trial. We place brands inside conversations, not on banners — entertainment, wellness, food, travel, lifestyle, retail.

05

International

Beyond Karachi

The house has hosted and shaped experiences across borders — the same standard of curation, safety, and atmosphere, translated for rooms that are not ours until the night begins.

06

Wellness Socials

Galleries, clubs, workspaces

A holistic once-a-month rhythm: music, movement, and community for a curated list of attendees. Sports clubs, art rooms, coffee houses — always with guardrails, never with chaos.
